This work examines architectural choices for Vision Transformers to perform efficient pixel-wise regression.
Experiments study changing the attention mechanism, patch size, encoder design, decoder design, and gradient checkpointing.
The variants are compared in terms of their runtime, perceptual quality, and quantitative performance.
